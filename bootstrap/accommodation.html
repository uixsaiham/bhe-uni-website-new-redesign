GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you
make your move.

Have another question? Our team can help you plan your accommodation search.

Talk to an Adviser
What does student accommodation include?

Options range from a furnished room in a shared residence to a self-contained studio. Facilities, kitchen access, internet, and utilities vary, so check the individual property listing before booking.

Is student accommodation a good idea?

Dedicated student accommodation can help you settle into a new city and meet other students. The right choice depends on your budget, campus location, and preferences.

Are bills included in student accommodation?

Some properties include utilities and internet in the rent, while others charge separately. Always check the listing and your tenancy agreement.

What facilities are available to student renters?

Properties may offer study areas, shared lounges, laundry facilities, gyms, or on-site support. Availability differs by residence.

When should I start looking for accommodation?

Start exploring once your study destination is clear. Popular rooms can fill up early, and booking ahead gives you more time to compare options and understand cancellation terms.

How can BHE UNI help me book?

Our advisers can help you understand your accommodation needs and guide you to Casita to explore properties and complete your booking with the provider.
