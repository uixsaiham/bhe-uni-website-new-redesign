Why Study in Greece

Greece has established public universities offering courses across business, engineering, medicine, archaeology and the humanities. When you study in Greece, institutions such as the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki and the University of Patras illustrate its academic diversity.

European qualifications, culture and academic choice

Greek higher education follows the European Higher Education Area and uses the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). You can explore university cities including Athens, Thessaloniki, Patras and Heraklion. Many undergraduate courses are Greek-taught, while selected international degrees are available in English.

A degree in Greece can provide academic experience in a distinctive Mediterranean setting. Programme language, professional recognition, accommodation and total costs all deserve careful comparison before choosing a university or course.

If you are unsure whether Greece suits your academic goals, this page explains its main study routes and what to check before applying.

Types of Study Routes in Greece

Greek universities offer undergraduate, postgraduate and research study, with language preparation where needed. Routes depend on your qualifications and the programme you choose.

Greek Language and Preparatory Study

May suit applicants who:

Plan to enter a degree taught mainly in Greek
Need to develop language skills before university study
Want to confirm academic eligibility and preparatory options

Language courses do not automatically lead to university admission. Check the Greek-language level and any entry procedure your chosen degree requires.

Undergraduate Degrees

Typical options include:

Greek-taught bachelor’s degrees at public universities
Selected international bachelor’s programmes taught in English
Degrees in sciences, humanities, technology or health

Many bachelor’s degrees follow a four-year structure, while certain subjects take longer. Admission channels and tuition arrangements differ by programme.

Postgraduate Master’s Degrees

For graduates seeking specialist study, routes include:

English-taught or Greek-taught master’s programmes
Specialist courses in business, technology or culture
Eligible joint international or Erasmus Mundus degrees

Master’s admission usually requires a relevant previous degree. Check programme language, official university requirements, tuition and academic deadlines.

Doctoral and Research Degrees

For research-focused applicants, options include:

Doctoral study at Greek public universities
Research projects with an appropriate academic supervisor

Doctoral requirements depend on subject, academic record and supervisory capacity. Confirm proposal expectations and available funding with the university.

Basic Entry Requirements for Studying in Greece

Entry rules depend on the university, programme language, degree level and your previous qualifications.

International bachelor’s applicants in Greece usually need recognised school qualifications. Applications for Greek-taught undergraduate courses may follow a special process set by the Ministry of Education. Selected international programmes use the @SiG application platform or the university’s own process. Postgraduate entry typically requires a suitable prior degree.

Many undergraduate courses are taught in Greek, so language preparation can be essential. Selected English-taught degrees in Greece require evidence accepted by the university, which may include IELTS, TOEFL or other qualifications. Requirements and exemptions differ. Always check the course’s teaching language and published criteria.

Compare published programme fees, accommodation and everyday expenses when planning your budget. Costs and possible fee exemptions depend on the course and student category. BHE UNI provides education and budgeting information, not visa or residence permit application services. For immigration rules, use official EU guidance on studying in Greece or a suitably authorised adviser.

Universities may ask for academic certificates, transcripts, proof of identity, language results and supporting documents such as a CV, references or a research proposal. Some routes also require officially translated or authenticated records. Check the Study in Greece information portal and your selected university for current document requirements.

Costs and Scholarships for Studying in Greece

Tuition fees and student living costs in Greece vary by course, qualification, student category and location.

Costs depend on:

Tuition fees depend on degree level, teaching language and programme arrangements. Some Greek-taught public undergraduate courses have different fee rules from English-taught international programmes. Always check the current university fee schedule.

Living costs depend on housing, food, transport and lifestyle. Athens, Thessaloniki and smaller university towns offer different budgets. Use official university guidance and the European Education Area Greece profile to estimate expenses.

BHE UNI supports you by:

Comparing published tuition charges for your selected Greek university programmes
Planning accommodation and living costs without relying on potential job income
Exploring university awards, Erasmus Mundus funding and relevant Greek scholarship calls

Scholarship schemes, including State Scholarships Foundation (IKY) opportunities, vary in eligibility and availability. Selection and financial support are not guaranteed.

Working While You Study in Greece

Some students may undertake part-time employment while studying in Greece, depending on their nationality and immigration status. The rules can change, and employment is not assured. Assess your budget without expected wages, and consult the competent authority or an authorised adviser for your own circumstances.

Our academic planning guidance focuses on:

University career centres and course-related placement information
Realistic financial planning independent of part-time earnings
Balancing academic commitments, language learning and employability

After You Graduate

A Greek university degree can support further education, research or professional development. Career options depend on qualifications, languages and professional requirements. Graduation does not guarantee employment or any right to remain; consult official authorities for current residence and employment rules.

Frequently Asked Questions about Studying in Greece

01Is Greece a good place to study for international students?

Greece offers established public universities, European degree structures and a growing choice of international programmes. The best fit depends on your subject, language skills, budget and preferred university. Compare verified courses and costs before deciding.

02Can international students study in Greece in English?

Yes. Selected Greek universities offer English-taught bachelor’s and master’s programmes, although many undergraduate degrees are taught in Greek. Language requirements, available subjects and admission routes differ between courses.

03What are the requirements to study in Greece?

Universities assess previous qualifications, language proficiency and programme-specific documents. Greek-taught undergraduate admissions may follow a Ministry of Education procedure, while international English-taught programmes may have separate applications. Always follow your chosen course’s instructions.

04How much does it cost to study in Greece?

Tuition fees vary by university programme, language, level and applicant category. Living costs depend on accommodation and city. Review the published tuition schedule and build a personal budget; a single estimate cannot represent every student.

05Are scholarships available for studying in Greece?

Some universities and international programmes offer scholarships, fee reductions or research funding. Eligible applicants can check Erasmus Mundus and relevant State Scholarships Foundation (IKY) calls. Availability, conditions and awards vary, so funding cannot be guaranteed.
