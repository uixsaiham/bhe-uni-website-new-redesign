Why Study in Cyprus

Located in the eastern Mediterranean, the Republic of Cyprus offers public and private higher education institutions. When you study in Cyprus, universities such as the University of Cyprus, Cyprus University of Technology and the University of Nicosia illustrate different academic pathways in business, computing, engineering and health.

European study opportunities and language choice

Cyprus participates in the European Higher Education Area, with qualifications organised across bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral study. Greek is widely used in public university teaching, while a selection of English-taught degrees in Cyprus is available. Students can compare opportunities in Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca and Paphos.

University status and course recognition are important. The Cyprus Agency of Quality Assurance and Accreditation in Higher Education (CYQAA) publishes accreditation information. Confirm your specific programme, teaching language and any professional recognition requirements before accepting an offer.

If you are unsure whether Cyprus suits your academic plans, this page explains study routes, recognition checks and what to compare before applying.

Types of Study Routes in Cyprus

Cyprus offers a range of university and higher education qualifications. Your options depend on prior study, academic interests and language. You can also compare courses in other study destinations before choosing.

Foundation and Preparatory Programmes

May suit applicants who:

Need English language or subject preparation
Have school qualifications requiring further assessment
Want to check recognised progression opportunities

Preparation courses do not guarantee a degree place. Confirm the course’s accreditation, progression arrangements and university entry criteria in writing.

Undergraduate Degrees

Typical options include:

Bachelor’s degrees at public or private universities
English-taught courses in selected subject areas
Specialist study at recognised higher education institutions

Entry conditions and programme accreditation vary. Check the university, degree title, teaching language and tuition policy for your applicant category.

Postgraduate Master’s Degrees

For students with a relevant first degree, options include:

Master’s programmes taught in English or Greek
Specialist degrees in technology, business or education
University-based research or professional pathways

Master’s applications normally require relevant academic qualifications. Individual programmes determine admission criteria, study mode, duration and costs.

Doctoral and Research Degrees

Research-focused students may explore:

Doctoral degrees with an approved research topic
Supervised projects at eligible universities

Research admission depends on academic preparation, supervisor availability and programme requirements. Funding and places are not guaranteed.

Basic Entry Requirements for Studying in Cyprus

Universities assess applicants according to degree level, programme, previous education and language of instruction.

Undergraduate applicants normally need appropriate school qualifications; postgraduate courses generally require a suitable previous degree. Public and private institutions use different selection procedures. Check the Department of Higher Education’s institution information and your chosen university. Certain professional courses impose additional conditions.

Check whether your programme is taught in English, Greek or another language. Evidence may include accepted school qualifications or recognised tests such as IELTS. Universities set their own minimum scores, exemptions and assessment procedures. English-language foundation study is a separate route; verify its accreditation and any progression conditions.

Compare published tuition fees, student accommodation and everyday living expenses. Fee arrangements can differ between EU and non-EU students, public and private universities, and qualifications. BHE UNI provides education and budgeting information, not immigration advice or residence-permit application assistance. For formal requirements, consult the European Commission’s Cyprus student information or an appropriately authorised adviser.

Universities may request transcripts, certificates, identification documents, language evidence, references or a personal statement. Research courses can require a proposal or academic supervisor approval. Rules for translations, certification and document formats vary. Confirm the latest university admissions checklist before submitting academic records.

Costs and Scholarships for Studying in Cyprus

Tuition fees and living costs depend on the university, degree, course arrangements and the student’s circumstances.

Costs depend on:

Tuition fees vary by institution, programme and nationality. Public undergraduate funding rules for eligible EU students differ from fees typically charged to non-EU applicants and many postgraduate students. Compare actual course prices rather than relying on a country-wide estimate.

Living costs include rent, food, utilities, health-related expenses and travel. Your budget may vary between Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca and Paphos. Use university estimates and the European Education Area’s Cyprus study profile when planning.

BHE UNI supports you by:

Comparing published tuition fees and payment policies for your shortlisted courses
Planning housing and everyday costs without depending on employment income
Checking university awards and general scholarship information alongside official eligibility rules

Cyprus scholarships, fee reductions and research funding depend on the institution and specific award. Some European opportunities, including Erasmus-related funding, have separate eligibility rules. No award is guaranteed.

Working While You Study in Cyprus

Some international students may be permitted to work while studying, but rights depend on nationality, student status and current employment conditions. Restrictions can apply to job types and working hours. Because opportunities are uncertain, calculate study costs without relying on wages and seek official guidance on personal eligibility.

Our academic planning guidance focuses on:

University careers services and published placement opportunities
Financial preparation without assumptions about part-time work
Balancing study, language development and professional skills

After You Graduate

A Cyprus qualification may support further education, research or a professional career, depending on the programme and its recognition. Entry to regulated professions and recognition abroad can involve additional requirements. Graduation does not guarantee a job or residence rights; refer to the competent authorities for current rules.

Frequently Asked Questions about Studying in Cyprus

01Is Cyprus a good place to study for international students?

The Republic of Cyprus offers public and private universities and a choice of academic programmes. Suitability depends on your subject, preferred language, budget and the specific programme’s accreditation. Compare verified information and university requirements before deciding.

02Can I study in Cyprus in English?

Yes. Selected universities offer English-taught bachelor’s, master’s and research programmes. Other degrees are taught in Greek. Check the teaching language and accepted English qualifications for the exact course, rather than assuming every programme is available in English.

03What are the entry requirements to study in Cyprus?

Requirements depend on the institution, degree level and subject. Universities may request school or degree certificates, transcripts, language evidence and supporting documents. Some regulated subjects have extra conditions. The chosen institution decides academic admission.

04How much does it cost to study in Cyprus?

Tuition fees differ between public and private institutions, qualifications and student categories, including EU and non-EU applicants. Living costs vary by location and accommodation. Use the university’s published fee schedule and build a personal budget.

05Are Cyprus university degrees recognised and are scholarships available?

Recognition depends on the institution, individual programme and the authority assessing the qualification. Check CYQAA records and relevant professional bodies. Universities may offer scholarships or fee reductions, but eligibility and funding are not guaranteed.
