KNOW WHAT COMES NEXT

Repaying your student loan.

Repayment arrangements depend on your loan plan and circumstances. Use the official repayment guidance to identify the rules that apply to you.

Thresholds and repayment amounts can change. Check your loan plan and current figures directly with the Student Loans Company. Interest and repayment calculations are separate.

About BHE UNI

BHE UNI is a trusted education consultancy supporting international, EU, and UK Home students with course selection, university admissions, scholarships, and visa-ready documentation where applicable. With offices in Bangladesh and the UK, we provide local expertise with global reach.
