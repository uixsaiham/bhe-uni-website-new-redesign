BHE UNI supports university admissions. Student finance eligibility and funding decisions are determined by the relevant funding body. We do not complete or manage student finance applications.

UNDERSTAND YOUR NEXT STEP

A second degree starts with clarity.

Check your study options and funding requirements before making a commitment.

What is a second-degree loan?

Previous higher education can affect access to student finance. Support for a further degree depends on the course and your circumstances.

Check the official eligibility guidance before assuming a second degree will qualify for funding.

Read official eligibility guidance

How does it work?

Students should check requirements and apply directly through the official student finance service.

The funding body decides whether support is available and how payments are made. A university offer does not guarantee funding.

Visit the official student finance service
YOUR CIRCUMSTANCES MATTER

Am I entitled to funding?

Ask the relevant funding body to assess your circumstances. These are the key details to confirm.

Check the official criteria
01

Your previous study

Your higher education qualifications and funding history.

02

Your exact course

The qualification, mode of study, and start date.

03

Your residence and circumstances

The funding rules for your part of the UK and personal situation.

How much can I borrow?

Amounts and types of support depend on the course, academic year, location, and individual assessment. Check current information with the funding body.

Read the official repayment guidance to understand the loan plan that applies to you.

Understand repayments

Which courses could qualify?

Do not assume that a subject or qualification automatically qualifies for student finance. Check your exact course and previous study with the relevant funding body.

Use official eligibility guidance and your university’s published course information when planning further study.

Check course eligibility

About BHE UNI

BHE UNI is a trusted education consultancy supporting international, EU, and UK Home students with course selection, university admissions, scholarships, and visa-ready documentation where applicable. With offices in Bangladesh and the UK, we provide local expertise with global reach.
